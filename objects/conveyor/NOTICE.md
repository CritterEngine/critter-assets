# Conveyor visual attribution

The polished conveyor visuals are adapted from “Conveyor” by Sketchfab creator
scailman, distributed through Objaverse under CC BY 4.0.

- Source model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/0819b51c59c3407cb98f0e2c75029e30
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: converted from GLB to OBJ, converted from Y-up to Z-up, normalized to a
  2.5 m longest dimension, assigned Critter materials, and combined with a custom
  MuJoCo conveyor mechanism and simplified collision geometry.
